BITCRM User Guide

A practical guide for sales, marketing, operations, and administrators.

Use it to understand what each module records, how modules work together, and where to complete each business action.

Complete bilingual guide · Updated August 6, 2026

1. Getting started

BITCRM connects the customer journey: who the customer is, whether an opportunity exists, what interaction happened, and what must happen next.

Simple rule: Leads and Pipeline describe who and what opportunity; Sales Activities describe what we did with the customer; Tasks describe what still needs to be done.

1.1 First login

  • Sign in with the username and initial password provided by the administrator.
  • Open the user menu at the top right and change the initial password as soon as possible.
  • Use 中文 / EN to switch the interface language. The user guide follows the selected language.
  • The left menu only shows modules available to your role and data scope.

1.2 Before entering data

  • Search for an existing company first to avoid duplicate Leads or opportunities.
  • Select the correct Owner. Ownership controls both responsibility and visibility.
  • Record completed communication as a Follow-up; record future action as Next Steps / To-do.
  • Use Cancel for a cancelled business action and Delete only when a record should be archived from normal views.

2. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the management overview. It summarizes current Leads, Pipeline value, forecasts, stages, and owner performance; it is not the place to edit detailed customer records.

2.1 Main indicators

IndicatorMeaningHow to use it
Total LeadsNumber of visible potential-customer records.Check whether the top of the funnel is large enough.
Qualified LeadsLeads confirmed as qualified.Review how many Leads are ready to become opportunities.
Pipeline #Number of visible sales opportunities.Review opportunity volume together with value and stage.
TCV / RevenueContract and revenue values calculated from Pipeline data.Use accurate Pipeline amount, term, signing, and activation dates.
Current / Next QTR RevenueQuarter-based forecast view.Update forecast dates when the customer timeline changes.

2.2 Pipeline Kanban and Sales by Owner

  • The Kanban board groups opportunities by stage. Move an item only when the business stage has genuinely changed.
  • Sales by Owner compares owner-level Pipeline results.
  • Owner selections for Dashboard views may be remembered by the browser. Use the relevant clear/reset action when you need the full view.
  • If a Dashboard number looks wrong, first check Pipeline status, amount, contract term, signing date, activation date, Owner, and current filters.

3. Sales Leads

Sales Leads stores potential customers before a clear sales opportunity is established. A Lead should contain enough information for another colleague to understand the customer, source, need, owner, and latest progress.

3.1 Create and maintain a Lead

  • Enter the company and contact information, Lead source, Lead status, requirements, and Owner.
  • Use Quick Edit for small field changes. Use the full edit page when several related fields must be checked together.
  • Use Follow-up Notes for communication that already happened.
  • Use Next Steps / To-do plus a due date for future work. This creates a linked Remote Engagement and Task.
  • A Lead saved as Qualified through manual creation, Excel import, Quick Edit, or the full edit page immediately creates or connects its Pipeline opportunity.

3.2 Find and review Leads

  • Filter by Company, Status, Source, and Owner; use Show All when normally hidden records must be reviewed.
  • Summary cards follow the current data scope and filters.
  • Column Settings changes which columns are displayed without changing stored data.
  • Follow-up History is the chronological customer record. Write the result and next decision, not only “contacted customer.”

3.3 Import, export, and archive

  • Download the import template before preparing a batch file. Do not rename required headers.
  • Review Owner, status, source, and duplicate companies after import. Every imported Qualified Lead is immediately transferred to Pipeline.
  • Export respects the module's available data and is useful for review, not as a replacement for updating CRM.
  • Delete archives the Lead from normal lists and preserves an audit snapshot; it is not an untraceable hard delete.

4. Pipeline

Pipeline manages qualified sales opportunities: expected service, commercial value, probability, timing, stage, support team, blockers, and communication history.

4.1 Important fields

Field groupBusiness purpose
Company / Contact / ServiceIdentifies the opportunity and what the customer may purchase.
MRC, OTC, contract term, TCVBuilds the commercial value used in summaries and forecasts.
Estimated Signing / ActivationPlaces expected revenue into the correct period.
Stage / Level / Win ProbabilityShows maturity, priority, and confidence. Keep the three logically consistent.
Owner / Support TeamDefines primary accountability and supporting access.
Current StuckpointExplains the main obstacle preventing progress.
Follow-up History / CommentsPreserves decisions, customer feedback, and context.

4.2 Working with opportunities

  • Filter by Company, signing or activation quarter, Stage, Level, and Owner.
  • Use Show All when reviewing Lost or other normally hidden historical stages.
  • Dragging a card in the Dashboard Kanban changes the business stage; confirm the customer situation first.
  • Use Follow-up for completed communication and Next Steps / To-do for the next dated action.
  • When a date, value, probability, or blocker changes, update Pipeline promptly so Dashboard forecasts remain useful.
  • Import, export, sorting, and Column Settings are available for batch work and review.

5. Sales Activities

Sales Activities is the execution record for customer interaction. Each record has a type, source, company, Owner, date or schedule, status, and optional linked Task.

5.1 Activity types

TypeUse it whenTime and completion rule
Customer VisitThe salesperson leaves the office to visit the customer's office, project location, event location, or another external site.Enter start and end date/time. Complete it either in Sales Activities or by entering required Completion Notes in the linked Task; status and feedback synchronize both ways.
DC Site VisitThe customer comes to our Data Center for a tour, technical discussion, security review, or facility inspection.Enter the reception schedule, Data Center address, visitors/contacts, purpose, and expected result. Complete it from Sales Activities or the linked Task; feedback synchronizes automatically.
Remote EngagementEmail, phone, online meeting, instant message, or another customer interaction that does not require a physical visit.Use Activity Date. Completed communication can be saved as Follow-up; future action becomes a linked Task through Next Steps / To-do.
DC Site Visit is not a Customer Visit: the customer comes to our Data Center. Record the Data Center location and customer visitors so reception, technical, security, and follow-up work remain clear.

5.2 Create an activity

1
Click Add Sales Activity and select one of the three activity types.
2
Select Source Type. For Sales Leads or Pipeline, choose a permitted record from the search results instead of typing only a company name.
3
Enter the schedule or Activity Date, contacts, purpose/project, expected result, and remarks.
4
Customer Visit and DC Site Visit are created only in Sales Activities. Sales Leads and Pipeline Follow-up can select an existing linked visit and complete it without creating a duplicate. New quick entries from those modules are Remote Engagement only.
5
A completed Remote Engagement Follow-up creates no Task. A new Remote Engagement Next Step creates a Scheduled Sales Activity and linked Task. Visits use their complete schedule and visitor details in Sales Activities.

5.3 Status and reminder logic

DisplayMeaning
ScheduledThe activity is planned and has not yet reached the time requiring follow-up.
Follow-up RequiredA visit has ended without feedback, or a Remote To-do has reached its due date.
Due TodayA detailed reminder that a Remote To-do is due today. It is counted inside Follow-up Required in summary tables.
OverdueA Remote To-do is past its due date, or a visit still has no feedback 24 hours after its end time. It is counted inside Follow-up Required in summaries.
CompletedThe result or required completion feedback has been recorded.
CancelledThe activity was cancelled with a reason and remains traceable.
Visit rule: both Customer Visit and DC Site Visit become Follow-up Required immediately after the end time. They become Overdue only when feedback is still missing 24 hours later.

5.4 Filters, calendar, summaries, and list

  • Type: All, Customer Visit, DC Site Visit, or Remote Engagement.
  • Date Range: Remote Engagement uses Activity Date. A visit is included whenever any part of its start/end interval overlaps the selected range, including cross-date visits.
  • Owner: administrators open one dropdown and tick one or more Owners. Every change applies automatically; there is no separate Filter button. All Owners clears specific selections.
  • Calendar: green indicates scheduled/completed activity, yellow follow-up/due today, red overdue, and gray cancelled/history. A blue date circle plus Today marks the current day without replacing the status background. Multiple calendar dates can be selected.
  • Activities by Owner: summarizes Scheduled, Follow-up Required, Completed, Cancelled, and Total. Due Today and Overdue are included in Follow-up Required totals.
  • Source Type by Owner: summarizes Sales Leads, Pipeline, Existing Customer, Event, Other, and Total.
  • Activity List: shows 20 records per page and preserves active filters during pagination.

5.5 Edit, complete, cancel, and archive

  • Open activities can be edited or rescheduled when the customer changes the time. The linked Task date, Owner, company, and content are synchronized.
  • Customer Visit and DC Site Visit can be completed in Tasks, Sales Activities, Sales Leads, or Pipeline with mandatory Completion Notes. Every entry updates the original Sales Activity and linked Task; Lead/Pipeline Follow-up never creates a duplicate visit.
  • Complete a Remote To-do in Tasks with mandatory Completion Notes; its Sales Activity updates automatically. “Open Sales Activity” is a detail-view shortcut, not a required path for completing a Visit.
  • Cancel keeps the record and reason. Delete archives the activity and its linked Task from normal views.
  • For Sales Leads and Pipeline sources, plans and feedback are appended to Follow-up History, including the linked Lead/Pipeline where applicable.

6. Tasks

Tasks is the operational work queue. It contains manual tasks and tasks generated by Sales Activities.

6.1 Task groups and actions

  • Overdue: unfinished work past its business deadline.
  • In Progress: active work that has not been completed or cancelled.
  • Completed: work finished with Completion Notes and completion information.
  • Cancelled: work intentionally stopped.
  • Use Owner filtering to focus the list. Non-administrators only see their own Tasks.
  • Completion Notes are required so colleagues can understand the actual result.
  • Reopen a completed or cancelled Task only when more work is genuinely required.

6.2 Linked-task rules

Task originWhere to complete or change it
Manual TaskManage directly in Tasks.
Remote Engagement — Next Steps / To-doComplete in Tasks or Sales Activities with Completion Notes; both records are synchronized.
Customer Visit or DC Site VisitCreate in Sales Activities. Complete it from Tasks, Sales Activities, Sales Leads, or Pipeline; all paths update the same activity and Task.
A Task linked to a Sales Activity cannot be independently edited or deleted in a way that breaks the activity record. Completion is available from Tasks or Sales Activities; use Sales Activities for rescheduling, activity-detail changes, or cancellation.
Historical-data compatibility: old Follow-up entries and Tasks created before rollout without a type or Sales Activity link remain unchanged. The system does not guess, backfill, or force them into Sales Activities. Only newly saved data with an explicit type uses the synchronization workflow.

7. Admin / User Management

User Management is available to administrators. It controls login identities, roles, activation status, and password resets.

  • Create a user with a unique username, email when required, role, and initial password.
  • Choose the role according to actual job responsibility; do not use Admin only to bypass normal visibility.
  • Deactivate an account when a user should no longer sign in. Historical ownership remains visible.
  • Use Reset Password when the user cannot recover access, then ask the user to change the temporary password.
  • Avoid deleting or replacing historical Owners solely for reporting convenience; preserving ownership supports audit and accountability.

8. Login Logs and Operation Archive

This administrator-only area is used to trace access and important operations. It is an audit view, not a page for editing customer data.

  • Filter by keyword, action type, username, start date, and end date.
  • Typical records include login, creation, update, follow-up, completion, cancellation, and archive/delete actions.
  • Use logs to answer who changed what and when, then verify the current business record in its source module.
  • Archived snapshots help investigate deleted records while keeping normal lists clean.

10. Recommended end-to-end workflow

1
Create or import the Sales Lead, confirm source, Owner, contact, and requirement.
2
Record each completed call, email, or meeting as Remote Engagement Follow-up. Add a dated Remote Engagement Next Steps / To-do when action is required.
3
When the Lead is qualified, maintain the Pipeline opportunity, value, stage, probability, timing, and blocker.
4
Use Customer Visit when we go to the customer; use DC Site Visit when the customer comes to our Data Center; use Remote Engagement for email, phone, or online meetings.
5
Complete Tasks with real outcomes and enter visit feedback promptly so Follow-up History stays current.
6
Review Dashboard, Activities by Owner, overdue work, and Pipeline blockers regularly.
Good record example: “Customer security team approved the DC access design; send the revised quotation by August 7, Owner: Alex.” It records result, next action, deadline, and responsibility.

12. Frequently asked questions

Which visit type should I choose?Choose Customer Visit when our staff go outside the office to the customer or project site. Choose DC Site Visit when the customer visits our Data Center. Choose Remote Engagement when no physical visit is required.
The customer changed the appointment time. What should I do?Open the unfinished activity and use Edit / Reschedule. The related Task is synchronized.
Why are Due Today and Overdue also included in Follow-up Required?They are detailed deadline reminders inside the broader business state “Follow-up Required.” Summary tables count them once under Follow-up Required, while the list shows the precise reminder.
Where do I create or complete a visit?Create Customer Visit and DC Site Visit in Sales Activities so all schedule and visitor fields are complete. You can complete an existing visit from Tasks, Sales Activities, Sales Leads, or Pipeline; the original activity and Task stay synchronized.
Why can I see only my own Activities by Owner row?Non-administrators are restricted to their own Sales Activities and Tasks. Administrators can review all Owners.
Can a cross-date visit be missed by Date Range?No. A visit is included whenever its scheduled interval overlaps the selected date range.
Why did a number change after filtering?Lists, summary tables, and many dashboard values follow the active data scope and filters. Clear filters before comparing totals.
Should I delete a cancelled customer meeting?Normally no. Cancel it and enter the reason so the business history remains understandable. Delete only when the record should be archived from normal use.
